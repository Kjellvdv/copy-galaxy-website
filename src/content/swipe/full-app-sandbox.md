[Mercury](https://demo.mercury.com/dashboard) runs a full copy of the product on its own subdomain, stocked with fake data. Real-looking balances, transactions, the whole sidebar.

Love it. No account, no email, no connecting a bank before you're allowed to look. You click the link and you're in. They've had it running for years, and there's a switcher for viewing it as an admin or as an employee.
