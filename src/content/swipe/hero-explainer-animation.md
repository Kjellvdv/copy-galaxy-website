[N.Rich](https://nrich.io/) swapped the static hero image for a 30-second animation showing what the product actually does, made by Josh Weale and Julia Kosova.

Their team measured it afterwards: clicks through to the pricing page went from 8 to 20 over the same period, from a similar number of sessions. The lever wasn't the words. Worth remembering next time you're stuck choosing between "robust" and "powerful".
