No longer live on Basecamp's site, but this one stuck with me. Instead of just stating a price, they add up what you'd pay for Slack, Asana, and Google Workspace separately and set that total next to Basecamp's flat fee. The comparison does the convincing, not the copy.

Clear, well designed, and a pattern I've gone back to for inspiration on several pages since.
