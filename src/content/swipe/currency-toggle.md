[N.Rich](https://nrich.io/pricing) puts a USD / EUR / GBP switch above the plan cards and redraws the numbers in place.

Neat, and the prices are properly set per currency rather than converted: $34,800 becomes €30,000, not €31,847.62. It saves a European buyer doing mental arithmetic on the one page where you least want them distracted.
