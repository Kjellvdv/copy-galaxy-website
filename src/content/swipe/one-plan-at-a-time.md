[Basecamp](https://basecamp.com/pricing) shows one plan at a time instead of lining four columns up side by side. Pick a tier on the left and the panel on the right fills in.

Cool because it's different, and losing the columns frees up the width to spell out what's actually included. Studio is flagged "Not sure? Start here", Pro gets "The sweet spot", and the largest tier sets off confetti.
